Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 31 Aviation, available at https://threeoneaviation.com, collects, uses and shares information when you visit the website.

This website is a static online photo catalogue supported by Google advertising. It does not require visitors to create an account, make a purchase or submit personal information.

The website is operated by 31 Aviation. Privacy-related questions and requests may be sent to info@threeoneaviation.com.

1. Information collected

We do not directly ask visitors to provide personal information through this website.

Limited technical and usage information may be collected automatically by the website's hosting provider, analytics provider, consent-management provider and advertising partners. This information may include:

  • Internet Protocol address;
  • browser type and version;
  • device type and operating system;
  • approximate geographic location;
  • referring website or page;
  • photographs or catalogue pages viewed;
  • date, time and duration of visits;
  • cookie identifiers and similar identifiers;
  • advertising impressions and interactions;
  • consent and privacy preferences; and
  • technical, diagnostic and security information.

Some of this information may be considered personal data under applicable privacy laws, even when it does not identify you directly by name.

2. How information is used

Information associated with visits to the website may be used to:

  • operate, maintain and secure the website;
  • display and organise the photo catalogue;
  • identify technical problems or abuse;
  • understand general website traffic and usage;
  • measure the performance of pages and photographs;
  • display, deliver and measure advertisements;
  • personalise advertisements where valid consent has been provided;
  • remember and communicate privacy choices; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use information collected through this website to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individual visitors.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

This website and its service providers may use cookies, web beacons, local storage and similar technologies.

Strictly necessary technologies may be used to operate and secure the website and to remember your privacy choices. Where required by law, optional analytics and advertising technologies are not activated until you make a choice through the website's consent-management platform.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent notice provided through a Google-certified consent management platform. This notice allows visitors to accept, reject or manage relevant advertising and analytics purposes.

You may review or change your consent choices using the website's privacy settings control.

Manage privacy settings

You may also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect certain website or advertising functions.

4. Google advertising

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements.

Google and other participating advertising technology providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalise and measure advertisements. Personalised advertising is used only where permitted by applicable law and your consent choices.

When your browser connects to Google advertising services, information provided to Google may include:

  • your IP address;
  • the URL of the page you are visiting;
  • browser and device information;
  • cookie or advertising identifiers;
  • advertising consent signals; and
  • advertisement impressions and interactions.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain its services and personalise advertisements where permitted.

You can review or change Google advertising personalisation settings through Google Ads Settings .

More information about Google's processing practices is available in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Depending on your location, consent choices and technical availability, Google may serve personalised, non-personalised or limited advertisements.

5. Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare to collect basic information about website traffic and performance.

Analytics information may include the pages or photographs viewed, approximate location, browser and device type, referring source and the date and duration of a visit.

This information is used to understand how visitors use the photo catalogue and to improve its content, organisation and technical performance.

Where consent is legally required, analytics technologies are used only after you provide consent through the website's consent-management interface.

6. Legal bases for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation, UK GDPR or similar legislation applies, personal data may be processed under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for optional analytics, advertising personalisation, cookies and similar technologies where consent is required.
  • Legitimate interests: for operating, maintaining and securing the website, preventing abuse and understanding basic website performance, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with applicable law or respond to a valid legal request.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time through the website's privacy settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred lawfully before consent was withdrawn.

7. Sharing of information

Information may be shared with:

  • Google and participating advertising providers;
  • the website's analytics provider;
  • the website's hosting provider;
  • security and technical-service providers;
  • professional advisers where reasonably necessary;
  • public authorities where disclosure is required by law or valid legal process.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual responsibilities. Google and the website operator may act as separate controllers for certain advertising-related processing.

We do not sell personal information directly or disclose it to unrelated third parties except as described in this policy or where required by law.

8. International data transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, including countries whose data-protection laws differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Where required, international transfers are made using recognised legal mechanisms, such as adequacy decisions, approved contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism.

9. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Information may be retained for a longer period where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce legal rights.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • obtain information about how your personal data is processed;
  • request access to your personal data;
  • request correction of inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive certain data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • lodge a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

These rights may be subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To submit a privacy request, contact info@threeoneaviation.com. You may be asked to provide sufficient information to verify your identity and identify the relevant data.

11. External websites

This website may contain links to external websites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those websites.

You should review the privacy policy of each external website you visit.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information associated with the website. However, no internet transmission, website or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Photographs and catalogue content

The photographs displayed in the catalogue form part of the website's published content. Viewing a photograph does not require you to provide personal information directly.

Questions concerning a photograph, its attribution or its removal may be sent to info@threeoneaviation.com.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's services, advertising providers, analytics tools or legal obligations change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
