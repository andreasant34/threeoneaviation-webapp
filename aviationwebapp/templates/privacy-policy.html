Privacy & Cookie Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use threeoneaviation.com and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. Who operates this website

ThreeOne Aviation operates this independent aircraft photography website. Privacy questions and data-rights requests can be sent to info@threeoneaviation.com.

You do not need an account and the site does not contain a contact form, shop or public comment system. If you email ThreeOne Aviation, the information in your message is used to answer and administer that correspondence.

2. Information processed when you visit

The hosting, content-delivery and security services used by this website may automatically process technical request data. This can include your IP address, browser and device type, requested URL, referring page, approximate location, request time and diagnostic or security information.

Cloudflare provides content delivery, security and basic traffic measurement. Its systems may process request data to deliver pages, prevent abuse and report aggregated site performance.

3. Google AdSense and cookies

This website contains Google AdSense code for site verification and advertising. When advertising is served, Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, page URLs, device or browser details, consent signals, cookie identifiers, ad impressions and interactions to deliver, limit, secure and measure advertising.

Where consent is legally required, optional advertising storage and personalised advertising are controlled through a Google-certified consent management platform. Visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland can consent, refuse or manage the available purposes. A Privacy and cookie settings link appears in the site footer when Google's consent API is available and can be used to reopen those choices.

Google explains its processing in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can also manage advertising personalisation in Google Ads Settings.

4. Why information is processed

  • To deliver the website and its aircraft photography;
  • to maintain security, diagnose faults and prevent abuse;
  • to understand aggregated traffic and improve navigation;
  • to verify, deliver and measure advertising;
  • to remember and communicate consent choices; and
  • to respond to messages, rights requests and legal obligations.

Depending on the processing and the law that applies, the legal basis may be consent, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the site, performance of a requested communication, or compliance with a legal obligation. Consent can be withdrawn without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. Service providers and international transfers

Information may be processed by the website's hosting and content-delivery providers, Cloudflare, Google and participating advertising providers, and professional or public authorities where disclosure is legally required. These providers handle data under their own terms and responsibilities.

Some providers operate internationally. Where required, transfers outside your jurisdiction rely on an applicable safeguard such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual clauses.

6. Retention

Technical logs and advertising data are retained according to the settings and retention practices of the relevant service provider and only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above. Email correspondence is retained while it is needed to answer the request, keep an appropriate business record, resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation.

7. Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, although this can affect site or advertising functions. Where the footer privacy-settings link is available, it can be used to revisit advertising choices.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Send a request to info@threeoneaviation.com. Enough information may be requested to verify your identity and locate the relevant data.

8. External links and photographs

This site links to external websites that have their own privacy practices. ThreeOne Aviation is not responsible for those sites. Viewing a catalogue photograph does not require you to submit personal information. Attribution, privacy or removal concerns about a published photograph can be sent to the contact address above.

9. Policy changes

This policy may change when the website, its providers or applicable requirements change. The current version and its update date will remain available on this page.
